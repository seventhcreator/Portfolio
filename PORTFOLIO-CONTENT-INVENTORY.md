## Professional case studies

### Path to El Dorado

Source: [case study 01](https://unreal-items-351168.framer.app/case-study-01).

- **Organization/context:** VirtualSoft; Doradobet account; B2B SaaS / iGaming engagement and retention.
- **Duration:** Eight months; calendar dates not supplied.
- **Challenge:** Attract new players and sustain engagement beyond the first weeks; many newly acquired/recently registered players churned within two months.
- **Personal contribution:** As Account Manager, translated the account owner's concern into a brief, brought marketing and commercial teams together, explained the pain point, facilitated brainstorming, managed the request pipeline, and presented the joint proposal to Doradobet. The learning section also describes keeping communication active and pushing backlog developments.
- **Concept:** An eight-month leaderboard tournament using a thematic journey through Ecuador. The source distinguishes the historical inspiration for its name from the campaign's modern route.
- **Route:** Otavalo → Quito → Cotopaxi → Quilotoa Loop → Baños de Agua Santa → Cuenca & Cajas → Guayaquil → Galápagos Islands.
- **Mechanism:** Players accumulated points through eligible casino/sportsbook activity, referral codes, winning streaks, and account top-ups.
- **Monthly rewards:** Five leaderboard leaders received rewards such as platform credit, football shirts, coupons, loyalty points, and gift cards.
- **Final rewards, in ranked order:** Three-day/two-night Galápagos trip; iPhone 14 Plus; PlayStation 5; USD 300 cash; USD 200 platform credit.
- **Reported retention result:** 20% → 55%, or **+35 percentage points**.
- **Reported activity result:** DAU and MAU initially rose by more than 35%, then settled at an average uplift of approximately 26%.
- **Reported revenue result:** Gross gaming revenue increased by 6% during campaign months.
- **Learning:** Shared problem-solving across talented teams; the personal contribution centers on coordination, client understanding, communication, and actionable proposals.

These are source-reported campaign results. The page does not provide measurement methodology, a control group, or separate baseline calculations; avoid stronger causal claims when drafting final copy.
